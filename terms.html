Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-11-10

These terms cover the X Post Radar Chrome extension and its Pro plans. By installing or using the extension, you agree to them.

1. The extension

X Post Radar scores the posts visible on your X timeline, in your browser. It does not use the X API, there is no X Post Radar account, and it never posts anything on X for you. The score is a decision aid, not a guarantee of reach or engagement. X can change its pages and ranking at any time, which can affect how the extension works.

2. Plans and billing

The extension has a free tier and a paid Pro tier. Pro is available on three plans:

PlanPriceBilling
Monthly$4.99Recurring monthly
Yearly$29.99Recurring yearly
Lifetime$49.99One-time

Pro is bought from the extension popup. Payments are handled by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com), which runs checkout on Stripe. The extension never sees or stores card data.

3. Cancellation and renewals

  • You can cancel a subscription at any time from the popup with Manage plan, which opens ExtensionPay's management page. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period, then the extension returns to the free tier.
  • If a renewal payment fails, Pro stays on while Stripe retries the payment, and the popup shows a warning.

4. Refunds

  • Refunds are available on request within 14 days of a charge (first purchase or renewal), for any plan including Lifetime. Email __SUPPORT_EMAIL__ with the email address you used for the purchase.
  • After a refund, the extension returns to the free tier at its next license check.
  • Outside 14 days, cancel to stop future charges; the current period is not refunded unless required by law.

5. Restoring a purchase and license checks

  • Restore purchase in the popup opens ExtensionPay's login page, which emails you a sign-in link, so you can use Pro on another browser.
  • The extension checks your license with ExtensionPay. If ExtensionPay cannot be reached, the last verified status is kept for up to 7 days before Pro turns off.

6. Your keys and third-party services

The optional TypeSafe score and Pro product promotion use your own TypeSafe and LLM API keys. Those requests are billed to your own accounts with those providers, and their terms and privacy policies govern what they receive. ExtensionPay's and Stripe's policies apply to the data they hold.

7. Your data

How the extension handles data is described in the privacy policy.

8. Provided as is

The extension is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind.

9. Changes and contact

Changes to these terms are published on this page with a new date. For questions, billing and refund requests, email __SUPPORT_EMAIL__.
