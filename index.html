Two reasons to use it

Attention is all you have

Every post on your timeline asks for your attention, and more of the feed is noise, including replies that read as AI-written. X Post Radar marks the posts worth reading and replying to, and can hide likely AI posts, so your time goes to people and conversations that matter to you.

Grow on X, one good reply at a time

A reply on the right post is read by that post's audience, not only by your followers. The radar points you to fresh, fast-moving posts in your niche, where a thoughtful reply has the best chance of being seen and of making people curious about your profile.

Most replies land where few people read them

Your timeline shows every post the same way. It does not tell you which ones are still gathering readers and which conversations have already moved on, so you pick by feel.

You spend ten minutes on a careful reply to a post from this morning. By then the conversation is over.

You reply to a large account, but the thread already holds hundreds of replies. Yours lands near the bottom.

A fresh post in your niche is picking up likes fast, and you scroll past it because nothing marks it.

Guessing costs you every session

Each reply takes the same effort whether a handful of people see it or thousands. Picking the wrong posts adds up.

  • Hours of writing go to threads that have stopped moving.
  • Finding the right post turns into a scrolling job before you write a word.
  • Off-topic posts and posts that read as AI-written compete for the same attention as the conversations you care about.

A 0–100 score on every post, computed in your browser

How the score works, and what leaves your browser

The score answers one question: how likely is it that your reply to this post is widely seen? Relevance gates, reach scores.

Signals in the score

  • Reach velocityHow fast the post is gaining likes and retweets, per hour. Highest default weight.
  • Author reachThe size of the author's audience, read from the timeline X has already loaded. Next-highest weight.
  • Reply windowHow fresh the post is.
  • Discussion intentWhether the post invites discussion.
  • Reply ratio
  • Keyword matchYour niche keywords, used as a relevance filter.
  • Author verificationWhether the author is verified.

A post with almost no engagement cannot reach High. A reply thread already crowded for the post's reach is pushed down, because a reply landing 800th will not be read.

The score is a decision aid, not a guarantee of reach or engagement.

FAQ

Can I install it today?

Not yet. X Post Radar is not on the Chrome Web Store. Join the waitlist and you will get an email when it is listed.

Does my timeline leave my device?

Not by default. Local scoring reads the posts on the x.com pages you open and computes scores inside your browser. Post text leaves only if you turn on the TypeSafe score with your own key, or use Pro product promotion. The table above lists exactly what is sent.

Do I need an X API key or an account with you?

No. The extension does not use the X API, and there is no X Post Radar account or server.

Will it post or reply for me?

No. Nothing is ever posted for you. Pro product promotion can draft a reply with your own AI key; you edit it, copy it and post it yourself.

What does the TypeSafe score cost?

X Post Radar does not charge for it on any plan. It uses your own TypeSafe API key, so requests are billed to your TypeSafe account. Results are cached per post to keep the cost down.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel any time from the popup with Manage plan; Pro stays active until the end of the paid period. For a refund within 14 days of a charge, email contact@luongnv.com with your purchase email address.

Does a high score guarantee reach?

No. The score is a decision aid, not a guarantee of reach or engagement, and X can change its pages and ranking at any time.
